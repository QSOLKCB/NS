# Literature Map

This file is a controlled intake map, not a claim that the listed topics are exhaustive.

## Canonical problem authority

- Clay Mathematics Institute, Navier–Stokes Equation: https://www.claymath.org/millennium/Navier-Stokes-Equation/
- Charles L. Fefferman, official problem description linked by CMI.

## Literature buckets to maintain

1. classical existence of weak solutions;
2. local strong/smooth existence;
3. uniqueness regimes;
4. partial regularity;
5. continuation and regularity criteria;
6. scaling-critical spaces;
7. vorticity and vortex-stretching geometry;
8. self-similar and discretely self-similar scenarios;
9. concentration/compactness and blow-up exclusion;
10. computational singularity diagnostics;
11. computer-assisted proof methods for PDEs;
12. related equations or modified Navier–Stokes models, clearly segregated from the canonical problem.

## Intake record for each source

Record:

- full citation;
- DOI/arXiv/publisher URL where applicable;
- theorem/result used;
- exact hypotheses;
- relation to the official problem;
- whether reproduced or independently checked;
- notes on limitations;
- whether the source blocks, supports or merely resembles a current research direction.

## Novelty rule

Do not use `new`, `novel`, `first`, `previously unknown` or equivalent language until the nearest known results have been identified and compared at the level of hypotheses and conclusion.
